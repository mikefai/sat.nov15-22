# Digital SAT Error Log & Daily Reflection Workbook

> **Why This File Drives Your 1200 → 1400+ Climb:** Doing 500 questions without diagnosing *why* you missed them repeats the exact same score. Every missed or guessed question is diagnostic data. Spend **15–20 minutes at the start of every study day** re-solving flagged items from this log without looking at the explanation first.

---

## 1. Official Error Classification Taxonomy

Never write vague reasons such as *"I didn't understand"* or *"Hard question"* in Column 5 (`Why I Missed It`). Always combine **one Primary Error Category** with (when applicable) **one Vocabulary/Language Sub-Label**:

### A. Ten Master Error Categories (Use Across R&W and Math)

| Code | Category | Exact Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `E1` | **Concept gap** | Did not know the underlying math formula, theorem, or rhetorical principle. |
| `E2` | **Vocabulary gap** | Unfamiliar academic word or secondary word meaning blocked comprehension or answer selection. |
| `E3` | **Complex sentence misunderstanding** | Lost track of the main subject-verb-object because of long modifiers, appositives, dashes, or inverted syntax. |
| `E4` | **Misread question** | Solved for `x` instead of `2x + 3`, missed *EXCEPT / NOT / LEAST*, or misread *weakens* as *supports*. |
| `E5` | **Incorrect inference** | Chose an answer that stretched beyond the text ("too extreme," "unsupported causal leap," or outside assumption). |
| `E6` | **Grammar rule forgotten** | Misapplied sentence boundaries (comma splice), subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, pronouns, or modifiers. |
| `E7` | **Careless mistake** | Sign flip (`-3²` vs `(-3)²`), copying error, misreading axis units/scale, or clicking the wrong option letter. |
| `E8` | **Timing problem** | Spent >2.5 minutes stalling on one hard question or rushed the final 4–5 questions in a module. |
| `E9` | **Wrong strategy** | Expanded messy algebra by hand for 3 minutes when plugging in a point or graphing in Desmos took 20 seconds. |
| `E10` | **Calculator or Desmos misuse** | Parentheses typo, radians vs. degrees mismatch, zoomed-out window hiding a second intersection, or misreading vertex coordinates. |

### B. Seven Vocabulary- & Language-Specific Error Labels (Mandatory for R&W Language Misses)

| Code | Vocabulary / Language Label | Diagnostic Clue |
|---|---|---|
| `V1` | **Did not know word meaning** | Target word in the stem, passage, or correct answer was completely unfamiliar. |
| `V2` | **Knew word but selected the wrong meaning** | Fell for a common everyday meaning when the SAT tested a secondary academic sense (e.g., *check* = restrain; *qualify* = limit; *arrest* = stop). |
| `V3` | **Misunderstood sentence structure** | Knew the individual words, but modifiers, participial phrases, or double negatives scrambled who did what to whom. |
| `V4` | **Missed a contrast or cause-and-effect clue** | Overlooked a pivot signal (*nevertheless, notwithstanding, albeit, conversely, consequently, whereas*) that dictated the blank's logic. |
| `V5` | **Confused similar answer choices** | Narrowed to 2 choices with similar positive/negative tone (e.g., *tentative* vs. *ambiguous*, or *corroborate* vs. *assert*) and picked the imprecise match. |
| `V6` | **Misunderstood the author's tone** | Confused neutral analytical description with strong praise/attack, or missed hedged academic caution (*suggests, may, plausible*). |
| `V7` | **Failed to connect evidence to the claim** | Read the passage and quote accurately at the surface level, but chose data/text that proved the *wrong* part of the hypothesis. |

## 2. The "3+ Recurring Pattern" Escalation Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Distinguish an Underlying Root Weakness from Broad Domain Coincidence:**
> - **NOT a single recurring pattern:** Missing 3 questions in `Craft and Structure` where one was an unknown noun (`V1`), one was a cross-text main-claim misread (`E4`), and one was running out of time (`E8`). Those are three separate issues.
> - **TRUE priority recurring pattern (Flag Immediately!):** Missing 3 questions across *different* topics (e.g., 1 `Words in Context`, 1 `Transitions`, 1 `Inferences`) because all three times you **missed a concession pivot (`V4`)** such as *nevertheless*, *albeit*, or *regardless*.
>
> **Action Rule:** Whenever the **exact same error code or underlying mechanism appears 3 or more times**, add it to the Priority Escalation Table below and dedicate the first 15 minutes of your next topic block to targeted repair before attempting mixed sets.
